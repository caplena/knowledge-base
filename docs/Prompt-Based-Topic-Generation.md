# Prompt-Based Topic Generation

So, you’ve uploaded your survey responses, NPS data, or customer reviews, and now it’s time to structure tyour data into clear, actionable themes. That’s where Caplena’s Topic Editor — and especially the Prompt-based Topic Generation feature — becomes your best friend.

Whether you're refining your existing topic set or building a new one from scratch, this guide will help you make the most of your tools — with AI support right when you need it, and you firmly in control.

Before diving into prompts, let’s clarify how Caplena typically helps you build your topic collection.

**Normal Topic Generation (a.k.a. Default AI Suggestions)**
When you open a project, Caplena scans your open-ended data and automatically suggests:

- Relevant categories (e.g., Price, Quality, Performance)

- Specific topics within each category (e.g., Picture Quality, Affordability)


![Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 12.04.06.png](<../assets/images/Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 12.04.06.png>)


It’s a great place to start. But sometimes you want to dig deeper, take control, or explore different angles. That’s where Prompt-based Topic Generation comes in. You’ll find the Prompt-based topic generation toggle right at the top of the Topic Editor:

![Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 12.33.55.png](<../assets/images/Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 12.33.55.png>)

This feature lets you talk to the AI directly using natural language. Think of it as your smart assistant who understands instructions like:

- “Give me only topics related to contracts”
- “Group feedback into categories: Price, Features, Service, and Other”
- “Find all brand names mentioned”
- “Suggest new themes based on smart TV features”

**It’s perfect when:**


1. You want to explore a specific angle in more depth

2. You’re missing a cluster of topics and need help generating ideas

3. You’re not satisfied with the initial AI output

## Playground vs. Entire Project
Once the feature is active, you can choose between two powerful modes:

 **Playground Mode** – Test without changing your project
This lets you experiment with prompts on a sample of the data. It’s safe, quick, and great for brainstorming.

**Use Playground if you:**

- Want to try ideas without affecting your live project

- Prefer to manually review and add new topics yourself

**🧠 Example:**

“Return all topics about features.” Caplena will suggest relevant themes — which you can then choose to keep or discard.

![Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 13.26.09.png](<../assets/images/Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 13.26.09.png>)


**Entire Project Mode** – Regenerate everything. Ready for a complete refresh? This mode runs your prompt across the entire dataset and rebuilds your topic collection from scratch.

**Use this when:**

- You’re starting fresh

- You want to restructure your entire topic collection based on your framework

🧠 **Example:**

“Group feedback under: Pricing, Usability, Performance, Design.”
Caplena will organize all discovered themes into these four categories.

![Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 13.23.02.png](<../assets/images/Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 13.23.02.png>)

### “Only” vs. “Also” — Choose Your Prompt Scope

These two buttons control how strictly Caplena follows your prompt:

![Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 13.45.29.png](<../assets/images/Screenshot 2025-06-05 at 13.45.29.png>)


 **Only**

Topics are generated solely based on your prompt, making it easier to focus on specific themes.

Use this when you want to narrow in on a topic set, like:

> “Only return contract-related issues.”

Perfect for deep dives.

**Also**

Topics are generated based on your prompt plus common themes from the project.

Use this when you want:

- A broader picture

- A mix of your focus + AI-discovered gems

>“Explore installation issues and also show any other top-mentioned topics.”

Great when you’re still exploring or don’t want to miss anything.
